## [1.0.0] - 2026-11-13

The first release of Specimen.

- Your pet is an envoy from another planet, and you are the xenologist observing it. Six home planets and 135 breeds with temperament, usual weight and health predispositions.
- One-tap logging of mood, appetite, weight, stool, symptoms and notes; red flags that mean «see a vet today».
- Life-support protocols made for the breed, with reminders; experiments with a hypothesis and a result; the diet ledger.
- The ethics committee refuses pranks, punishment, hunger and anything poisonous to the species.
- The behaviour atlas: 200 poses and habits opened by your own photos, each with what it means.
- A typed weekly report with your photos, captions, the behavioural profile and conclusions, shared as a picture; four designs bought with grants.
- The xenology course: 45 lessons with field tasks and a question; ranks from laboratory assistant to academician.
- A PDF extract for the vet; backup to a zip file; light and dark themes in the style of an old star atlas.

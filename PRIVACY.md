# Privacy

Specimen has no account, no analytics and no server. Your specimens, observations, experiments, life-support log, photos, lessons and reports stay on your phone, in the app's own storage. Android's backup may keep a copy, as with any app, if you turned it on; a backup file you save yourself goes wherever you put it.

**A second phone.** If you pair a second phone (Settings → Second phone), the two find each other only on your local network – by mDNS and a broadcast on the home Wi-Fi – and talk directly. Everything between them is encrypted with AES-GCM under a key that passed once through the QR code; nothing goes to the internet or to anyone else. The camera is used only to scan that QR. In the background the link runs only if you turn it on, and only on the network where the two phones have met.

**Photos** are taken with your phone's own camera app or picked from your gallery, and a copy is kept in Specimen's private storage (and on the paired phone, if you have one).

**Reports and vet extracts** leave the phone only when you share them yourself.

**Reminders** are local notifications; nothing is sent anywhere to schedule them.

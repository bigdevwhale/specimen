# Privacy

Specimen has no account, no analytics and no internet access at all. Your specimens, observations, experiments, life-support log, photos, lessons and reports stay on your phone, in the app's own storage. Android's backup may keep a copy, as with any app, if you turned it on; a backup file you save yourself goes wherever you put it.

**Photos** are taken with your phone's own camera app or picked from your gallery, and a copy is kept in Specimen's private storage.

**Reports and vet extracts** leave the phone only when you share them yourself.

**Reminders** are local notifications; nothing is sent anywhere to schedule them.
